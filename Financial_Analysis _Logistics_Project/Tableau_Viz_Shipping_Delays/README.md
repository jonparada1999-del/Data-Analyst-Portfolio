Supply Chain Financial & Logistics Audit

Tools: SQL (BigQuery), Tableau, Data Modeling

Link: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jonathan.parada/viz/ShippingDelays_17713552279380/RegionalProfitabilityLeakageAnalysis#2

Project Overview: I developed this suite of visualizations to identify the intersection between logistics performance and financial health. By analyzing shipment volumes, regional profitability, and delivery efficiency, I can pinpoint exactly where the supply chain is losing money.

1. Regional Profitability & Leakage Analysis
Goal: I built this to identify which global regions are driving the highest returns and which are suffering from "profit leakage."

My Insight: I categorized profit by region to help the finance team see that while Western Europe leads in total profit, smaller regions like Central Asia and Eastern Africa require more streamlined operations to become viable.

2. Late Shipment Risk by Shipping Mode
Goal: I created this bar chart to quantify the operational risk associated with different fulfillment methods.

My Insight: I discovered that 'Standard Class' accounts for the highest volume of late shipments (over 42k). This data suggests that our most-used shipping method is also our highest-risk method for customer dissatisfaction.

3. Shipping Efficiency: Real vs. Scheduled Days
Goal: I designed this scatter plot to visualize the variance in our logistics timeline.

My Insight: By plotting real shipping days against scheduled days, I can identify outliers where shipping took longer than promised. This serves as a "ground truth" for auditing our logistics partners.
